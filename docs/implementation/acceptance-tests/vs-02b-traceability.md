# VS-02b traceability matrix

Every VS-02b acceptance criterion bullet and VS-02b contract check, with the automated tests that cover it ([TEST-003](../tasks/TEST-003.md) §5, routine item 1; the form is [D063](../../product/decisions/D063-vs02-traceability-and-budgets.md)'s, as vs-02b.md §6 item 15 set). The [VS-01](vs-01-traceability.md) and [VS-02](vs-02-traceability.md) matrices stay enforced (AC2b-08). `backend/tests/test_traceability.py::test_every_vs02b_item_is_traced` enforces this file:

- every item of the three source lists has exactly one row (33 in all);
- each row's criterion is the source's text, after normalizing whitespace, bold markers, and Markdown links to their text. Rewording a criterion fails until its row, and the tests listed for it, are reviewed again;
- every row lists at least one test, or starts with `N/A — ` and gives a reason;
- every referenced test exists, is not skipped, and a Vitest or Playwright title occurs once in its file, as the title of `it`, `test`, or `describe` (TEST-002 §5.3).

**Reference forms** are the VS-01 and VS-02 matrices':
- pytest: `backend/tests/<file>.py::<function>`. A parametrized test is referenced by function; a short note after the reference names the case or step that matters, and the check ignores it;
- Vitest and Playwright: `frontend/<path> › <exact test title>`. Playwright cases inside `test.describe(…)` are referenced by their own titles, because the check accepts only `it(`, `test(`, or `describe(` (TEST-003 §9).

A test being fixed may be marked `[quarantined: <issue>]` (TEST-001 §8). A row whose every reference is quarantined fails the check.

**Sources:**
- AC2b: [vs-02b.md §15](vs-02b.md#15-acceptance-criteria-approved). A criterion with bullets has one row per bullet, numbered in order (`AC2b-02.1`); a criterion without bullets is one row;
- ART2b: [artifacts.md §10](../../architecture/artifacts.md#10-acceptance-tests-additions-to-vs-01), "VS-02b reconciliation", bullets in order;
- DEP2b: [dependencies.md §8](../../architecture/dependencies.md#8-vs-01-examples-and-tests), "VS-02b examples", bullets in order.

**Integration tests** (`backend/tests/integration/`) check the whole house after every step, so they appear in many rows. The golden scenario (`test_reference_house_scenario_matches_golden`) sends the four wall commands with a rejection of each, one of them a fit rejection through `SetWallAssembly` (TEST-003 §6). The sequences (`test_random_command_sequences_keep_every_invariant`) draw the four wall commands, check exact impact over five artifacts at every step, check that walls, wall framing, and openings keep their identity and that no opening moves silently, and under the `ci` profile an outcome gate fails if they stop reaching an outcome TEST-002 §7 or TEST-003 §7.4 requires.

**CI** (AC2b-11) runs `scripts/check.sh`'s steps, so every test referenced here runs there. The budgets are [D063](../../product/decisions/D063-vs02-traceability-and-budgets.md)'s and [D090](../../product/decisions/D090-vs02b-budgets-and-named-wall-case.md)'s.
