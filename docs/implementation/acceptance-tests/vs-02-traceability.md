# VS-02 traceability matrix

Every VS-02 acceptance criterion bullet and VS-02 contract check, with the automated tests that cover it ([TEST-002](../tasks/TEST-002.md) §5, approved as item 6). The [VS-01 matrix](vs-01-traceability.md) stays as it is and is still enforced (AC2-05). `backend/tests/test_traceability.py::test_every_vs02_item_is_traced` enforces this file:

- every item of the three source lists has exactly one row (24 in all);
- each row's criterion is the source's text, after normalizing whitespace, bold markers, and Markdown links to their text. Rewording a criterion fails until its row, and the tests listed for it, are reviewed again;
- every row lists at least one test, or starts with `N/A — ` and gives a reason;
- every referenced test exists, is not skipped, and a Vitest or Playwright title occurs once in its file, as the title of `it`, `test`, or `describe` (TEST-002 §5.3).

**Reference forms** are the VS-01 matrix's:
- pytest: `backend/tests/<file>.py::<function>`. A parametrized test is referenced by function; a short note after the reference names the case that matters, and the check ignores it;
- Vitest and Playwright: `frontend/<path> › <exact test title>`;
- npm scripts: `frontend/package.json#<script>`.

A test being fixed may be marked `[quarantined: <issue>]` (TEST-001 §8). A row whose every reference is quarantined fails the check.

**Sources:**
- AC2: [vs-02.md §12.1](vs-02.md#121-vs-02-acceptance-criteria-revised-for-d051d056-these-replace-5). A criterion with bullets has one row per bullet, numbered in order (`AC2-02.1`); a criterion without bullets is one row;
- ART2: [artifacts.md §10](../../architecture/artifacts.md#10-acceptance-tests-additions-to-vs-01), "Version 2 reconciliation", bullets in order;
- DEP2: [dependencies.md §8](../../architecture/dependencies.md#8-vs-01-examples-and-tests), "VS-02 examples", bullets in order.

**Integration tests** (`backend/tests/integration/`) check the whole house after every step, so they appear in many rows. The golden scenario (`test_reference_house_scenario_matches_golden`) covers every command, `SetFrameParameters` and `SetConnectionType` included, with a rejection of each (TEST-002 §6). The sequences (`test_random_command_sequences_keep_every_invariant`) check the if-and-only-if impact property (AC2-04) at every step, and under the `ci` profile an outcome gate fails if they stop reaching an outcome TEST-002 §7 requires.

**CI** (AC2-09) runs `scripts/check.sh`'s steps, so every test referenced here runs there.
