# Product vision

Build a web-first architectural design platform that helps homeowners conceive affordable, sustainable, buildable homes and helps builders and professionals develop the technical information required to construct them. Design, analysis, estimating, and documentation operate on a single semantic, parametric building model.

The initial construction specialty is traditional heavy-timber post-and-beam houses, with traditional joinery and modern engineered connectors, and modular building-envelope assemblies including straw bale and stud-wall infill. Vermont is the first target jurisdiction; expansion to New England and beyond must be supported architecturally.
