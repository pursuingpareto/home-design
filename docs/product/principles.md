# Foundational principles

1. **Design freedom:** Creative exploration without unnecessary technical barriers.
2. **Buildability:** Continuously track physical and construction implications; unsupported designs must be identified.
3. **Transparency:** Explain constraints, assumptions, uncertainty, and alternatives. Do not silently redesign.
4. **One model:** 2D, 3D, AI, analyses, and deliverables share the same authoritative building data.
5. **Human control:** AI changes are explicit proposals, inspectable, reversible, and subject to validation.
6. **Traceability:** Computations, estimates, approvals, and generated documents reference a specific model revision and inputs.
7. **Honest assurance:** Proposed, automatically analyzed, and professionally verified designs are distinct statuses.
8. **Portability:** Specifications and implementation tasks are independent of coding-agent vendors.
